    The accuracy trap
    Only % of the 23,452 windows are attacks. A model that always says "normal" scores 98.1% accuracy and catches nothing. Look at recall and precision instead. A depth-5 decision tree on a chronological split reaches recall near 0.95 and precision near 0.78 offline.
    
  
    Honest limits
    Security risk and operational health are statistically unrelated, except for A-001 and effects built into exposure and criticality. Do not let anyone claim that weak security causes equipment faults from this data.

    Guardrails for everyone
    
 
    
  
    Data is synthetic
    No real ENEC data, names, systems or incidents are in any file. Do not paste real plant data into Copilot, ChatGPT or any notebook.
    
  
    Check before you trust
    Compare each AI answer with its check answer or a filter you can see. Cite the document and version. Say supported, contradicted or unknown.
    
  
    A person decides
    AI proposes, a person approves. No exercise acts on a control system. Agents on Day 3 can only read and recommend.
    
  
    Keys stay secret
    The OpenAI key goes in Colab Secrets, never in a notebook cell or on GitHub.
    
  
    Attack answer key
    The file listing attack episodes is for instructors. Decide whether it stays out of the public repository.
    
  
    Official accounts
    Copilot is used with official Microsoft accounts only. Colab uses a personal Google account.
