1. Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the minimum evidence, review and escalation expectations for condition monitoring, 
maintenance prioritization and AI-assisted asset reliability workflows across the fictional Northstar 
Process Facility. It applies to all rotating and fixed assets, including A-001.
2. Reliability Principles
 Separate observed measurements from interpretation and recommended action.
 Use the current approved procedure when a document governs an inspection or maintenance task.
 Do not treat similarity, model confidence or an automated risk score as authorization to act.
 Escalate when evidence conflicts, the applicable document is unclear, or the condition exceeds the local
review threshold.
 Record the source, version, timestamp and reviewer for material decisions.

5. AI-Assisted Reliability Controls
1. Retrieve evidence before generating a recommendation.
2. Apply authority and scope filters before selecting final context.
3. Cite every material claim to a source passage or structured record.
4. State when evidence is missing, conflicting or stale.
5. Require a human reviewer for maintenance authorization, shutdown decisions or safety-related 
interpretation.
6. Escalation Record
An escalation record should include the asset ID, observed condition, supporting measurements, open 
work orders, current procedure reference, unresolved gaps, proposed review owner and approval status. 
AI-generated text may summarize evidence but must not invent missing evidence or approvals.
7. Example - A-001
A-001 shows a sustained vibration increase while an inspection work order remains open. The correct 
workflow is to verify the measurement trend, retrieve the current approved inspection procedure, review 
recent findings and work history, and then request qualified human review. The policy does not itself 
prescribe a technical repair.
